How do I protect my brand name? Trademark basics in India

File online with IP India for ₹4,500 a class if you are an individual, startup or small business. What registration protects, and what ™ and ® mean.

A trademark is the name, logo or slogan that tells customers a product or service comes from you. Using a mark gives you some protection, but registration under the Trade Marks Act, 1999 gives you a statutory right you can enforce across India.

  • Search first. Use the public search on the IP India website to look for identical or similar marks in your class of goods or services. A clash with an earlier mark is the most common reason for objections.
  • File Form TM-A online. The government fee is ₹4,500 per class for individuals, DPIIT-recognised startups and small enterprises filing online, and ₹9,000 per class for others. Goods and services are divided into 45 classes, and you pay for each class you claim.
  • Examination, publication, opposition. The Registry examines the application and may raise objections that you must answer. If it is accepted, the mark is advertised in the Trade Marks Journal, and anyone can oppose it within four months.
  • Ten years, renewable. A registration lasts ten years and can be renewed again and again for further ten-year terms.
  • ™ and ®. You may use ™ with any mark you claim. Use ® only after registration: falsely representing a mark as registered is an offence under Section 107 of the Act.

Search before you spend on a logo. A name you cannot register is a name you may have to give up.

Common questions

Do I need to register a trademark to stop someone copying it?

No. An unregistered mark can be protected in court through a passing-off action, but you must prove goodwill, misrepresentation and damage. Registration makes enforcement easier because an infringement claim under Section 29 does not require you to prove goodwill.

Can I register a common word or a place name?

Marks that only describe the product, its quality or its geographical origin are generally refused under Section 9, unless they have become distinctive of your business through use.

Can I use my brand before registration comes through?

Yes. You can use the mark with ™ while the application is pending. Expedited examination is available for a higher fee if you need a decision sooner.
