When does an autonomous body become “State”?

The Supreme Court held that the National Book Trust is “State” under Article 12.

Autonomous bodies, societies and trusts often do public work. Whether they are bound by fundamental rights, and by writ jurisdiction, turns on Article 12. The Supreme Court has now held that the National Book Trust is “State” (Chairman, National Book Trust v. A.R. Abdul Gaffar, 2026 INSC 1202, 6 November 2026), applying Ajay Hasia (1981) and Pradeep Kumar Biswas (2002).

  • Scope. “State” includes “local or other authorities”, not just government departments.
  • The test. Is government control merely regulatory, or deep and pervasive, financially, functionally and administratively?
  • Three markers: Substantial State funding; government control over the governing body, appointments and rules; and public functions closely tied to State objectives.

Wherever public power is exercised, constitutional accountability applies.
