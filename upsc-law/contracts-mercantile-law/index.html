Law of Contracts and Mercantile Law: UPSC Law Optional notes and PYQs

All 13 syllabus units of Law of Contracts and Mercantile Law, in syllabus order. Each unit page shows how to decode the question, frame the answer, which provisions and cases to cite, and every previous-year question from 2013 to 2026 (113 questions in all).

UPSC Law Optional desk · Paper II

Unit 1 · Asked 12 times · 2013–2026

Formation of contract and e-contracts

Questions test offer and acceptance (including e-contracts) and privity with its exceptions. Use the Contract Act's illustrations.

Cases: Carlill v. Carbolic Smoke Ball Co. (1893), Lalman Shukla v. Gauri Dutt (1913), Bhagwandas Goverdhandas Kedia v. Girdharilal Parshottamdas (1966)

Notes, answer frame and questions →Unit 2 · Asked 7 times · 2013–2026

Free consent: coercion, undue influence, fraud, mistake

Questions test coercion, undue influence and mistake. For undue influence, state what the plaintiff must prove and when it is presumed.

Cases: Chikkam Ammiraju v. Chikkam Seshamma (1917), Raghunath Prasad v. Sarju Prasad (1923, PC), Subhas Chandra Das Mushib v. Ganga Prasad Das Mushib (1967)

Notes, answer frame and questions →Unit 3 · Asked 13 times · 2014–2025

Void, voidable and illegal agreements

Questions test void vs voidable vs illegal, minors' agreements, restraint of trade and wagering.

Cases: Mohori Bibee v. Dharmodas Ghose (1903, PC), Gherulal Parakh v. Mahadeodas Maiya (1959), Nordenfelt v. Maxim Nordenfelt (1894)

Notes, answer frame and questions →Unit 4 · Asked 7 times · 2015–2025

Performance, discharge and frustration

Questions test frustration (s. 56) and modes of discharge. Separate frustration from force majeure.

Cases: Satyabrata Ghose v. Mugneeram Bangur (1954), Taylor v. Caldwell (1863), Krell v. Henry (1903)

Notes, answer frame and questions →Unit 5 · Asked 4 times · 2019–2025

Quasi-contracts

Questions test the basis of quasi-contracts and ss. 68–72.

Cases: Moses v. Macferlan (1760), State of West Bengal v. B.K. Mondal & Sons (1962), Sales Tax Officer v. Kanhaiya Lal Mukundlal Saraf (1959)

Notes, answer frame and questions →Unit 6 · Asked 4 times · 2014–2024

Breach of contract, damages and specific relief (s. 73–75)

Questions test ss. 73–74 and remoteness. Use Hadley's two limbs and Fateh Chand.

Cases: Hadley v. Baxendale (1854), Fateh Chand v. Balkishan Dass (1963), ONGC v. Saw Pipes (2003)

Notes, answer frame and questions →Unit 7 · Asked 8 times · 2013–2024

Indemnity, guarantee and insurance

Questions test indemnity vs guarantee, the surety's rights and discharge, and insurance.

Cases: Gajanan Moreshwar Parelkar v. Moreshwar Madan Mantri (1942), Bank of Bihar v. Damodar Prasad (1969), State Bank of India v. Indexport Registered (1992)

Notes, answer frame and questions →Unit 8 · Asked 9 times · 2014–2026

Contract of agency

Questions test the nature of agency (representative character, derivative authority), kinds of authority and liability.

Cases: Lakshminarayan Ram Gopal v. Govt. of Hyderabad (1954), Freeman & Lockyer v. Buckhurst Park Properties (1964), Watteau v. Fenwick (1893)

Notes, answer frame and questions →Unit 9 · Asked 13 times · 2014–2026

Sale of goods and hire purchase

Questions test sale vs agreement to sell, implied conditions, caveat emptor and hire-purchase.

Cases: Priest v. Last (1903), Grant v. Australian Knitting Mills (1936), Baldry v. Marshall (1925)

Notes, answer frame and questions →Unit 10 · Asked 11 times · 2013–2026

Partnership: formation and dissolution

Questions test the mutual-agency test, non-partners sharing profits, registration and dissolution.

Cases: Cox v. Hickman (1860), K.D. Kamath & Co. v. CIT (1971), Haldiram Bhujiawala v. Anand Kumar Deepak Kumar (2000)

Notes, answer frame and questions →Unit 11 · Asked 8 times · 2013–2023

Negotiable Instruments Act and s. 138 cheque bounce

Questions test holder in due course, presumptions and s. 138 litigation.

Cases: Rangappa v. Sri Mohan (2010), Dashrath Rupsingh Rathod v. State of Maharashtra (2014), Meters and Instruments v. Kanchan Mehta (2017)

Notes, answer frame and questions →Unit 12 · Asked 10 times · 2014–2025

Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996

Questions test arbitrability, court intervention and the role of ADR in public matters.

Cases: Bharat Aluminium Co. v. Kaiser Aluminium (2012), Booz Allen & Hamilton v. SBI Home Finance (2011), Vidya Drolia v. Durga Trading Corp. (2021)

Notes, answer frame and questions →Unit 13 · Asked 6 times · 2015–2026

Standard form contracts

Questions ask how courts protect weaker parties in standard form contracts.

Cases: L'Estrange v. Graucob (1934), Thornton v. Shoe Lane Parking (1971), Central Inland Water Transport Corp. v. Brojo Nath Ganguly (1986)

Previous-year questions (1)
  1. 2014X and Co. in its prospectus represented that A, B and C would be the directors of the company. This was true, and on the basis of this, P and Q applied for shares. However, before the allotment took place, there were changes in directors. Is the allotment of P and Q subject to their choice, or does it stand cancelled due to a change in directors? Discuss.
