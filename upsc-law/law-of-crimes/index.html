UPSC CSE Law Optional · Paper II

Law of Crimes: UPSC Law Optional notes and PYQs

All 12 syllabus units of Law of Crimes, in syllabus order. Each unit page shows how to decode the question, frame the answer, which provisions and cases to cite, and every previous-year question from 2013 to 2026 (108 questions in all).

UPSC Law Optional desk · Paper II

Unit 1 · Asked 8 times · 2013–2024

Mens rea and actus reus

Questions test the mens rea maxim, the concurrence of act and intent, and strict liability. Explain the maxim, show how the BNS builds mens rea into its definitions, then the statutory exceptions.

Cases: R v. Prince (1875), R v. Tolson (1889), Sherras v. De Rutzen (1895)

Notes, answer frame and questions →Unit 2 · Asked 5 times · 2013–2026

Kinds of punishment and capital punishment

Questions test the theories of punishment, the death-penalty debate and new forms such as community service. Pair penology with case law.

Cases: Bachan Singh v. State of Punjab (1980), Machhi Singh v. State of Punjab (1983), Mithu v. State of Punjab (1983)

Notes, answer frame and questions →Unit 3 · Asked 4 times · 2014–2021

Preparation and attempt

Questions test the line between preparation and attempt. State the four stages, the tests, then BNS s. 62.

Cases: R v. Eagleton (1855), Abhayanand Mishra v. State of Bihar (1961), State of Maharashtra v. Mohd. Yakub (1980)

Notes, answer frame and questions →Unit 4 · Asked 16 times · 2013–2026

General exceptions and private defence (BNS s. 14–44)

Very frequent. Private defence, insanity, intoxication and mistake recur. Classify each as justification or excuse, then apply.

Cases: State of Orissa v. Bhagaban Barik (1987), Chirangi v. State of Nagpur (1952), Dahyabhai Chhaganbhai Thakkar v. State of Gujarat (1964)

Notes, answer frame and questions →Unit 5 · Asked 4 times · 2017–2022

Joint and constructive liability (common intention, common object)

Classic: compare s. 34 and s. 149 (now BNS s. 3(5) and s. 190). A comparison table earns marks.

Cases: Barendra Kumar Ghosh v. King-Emperor (1925, PC), Mahbub Shah v. Emperor (1945, PC), Pandurang v. State of Hyderabad (1955)

Notes, answer frame and questions →Unit 6 · Asked 3 times · 2014–2024

Abetment

Questions test the modes of abetment and abetment of suicide. Use illustrations from the BNS.

Cases: Faguna Kanta Nath v. State of Assam (1959), Ramesh Kumar v. State of Chhattisgarh (2001), M. Mohan v. State (2011)

Notes, answer frame and questions →Unit 7 · Asked 3 times · 2015–2024

Criminal conspiracy

Questions test the ingredients of conspiracy and its proof. Distinguish it from abetment.

Cases: Kehar Singh v. State (Delhi Admn.) (1988), State v. Nalini (1999), Yash Pal Mittal v. State of Punjab (1977)

Notes, answer frame and questions →Unit 8 · Asked 41 times · 2013–2026

Offences against the State, body, property and women

The largest Paper II unit. Expect BNS changes (sedition to s. 152, theft, sexual offences), the murder vs culpable homicide distinction, and fact problems. Use IPC → BNS mapping in every answer.

Cases: Kedar Nath Singh v. State of Bihar (1962), S.G. Vombatkere v. Union of India (2022), Virsa Singh v. State of Punjab (1958)

Notes, answer frame and questions →Unit 9 · Asked 1 time · 2022

Criminal defamation

Questions test the exceptions to defamation and its constitutionality.

Cases: Subramanian Swamy v. Union of India (2016), R. Rajagopal v. State of T.N. (1994)

Notes, answer frame and questions →Unit 10 · Asked 9 times · 2014–2026

Prevention of Corruption Act, 1988

Questions test demand and acceptance, the 2018 amendments and fact problems. Apply s. 7, presumption under s. 20, and sanction.

Cases: Neeraj Dutta v. State (NCT of Delhi) (2022), B. Jayaraj v. State of A.P. (2014), P. Satyanarayana Murthy v. DIG of Police (2015)

Notes, answer frame and questions →Unit 11 · Asked 8 times · 2015–2026

Protection of Civil Rights Act and SC/ST Act

Questions trace the evolution of anti-untouchability law. Present it as a timeline from Art. 17 to the 2015 and 2018 amendments.

Cases: People's Union for Democratic Rights v. Union of India (1982), State of Karnataka v. Appa Balu Ingale (1993), Subhash Kashinath Mahajan v. State of Maharashtra (2018)

Notes, answer frame and questions →Unit 12 · Asked 6 times · 2013–2026

Plea bargaining

Questions test the safeguards ensuring voluntariness and the judiciary's role. Use BNSS sections.

Cases: Murlidhar Meghraj Loya v. State of Maharashtra (1976), Kasambhai Abdulrehmanbhai Sheikh v. State of Gujarat (1980), State of Gujarat v. Natwar Harchandji Thakor (2005)
