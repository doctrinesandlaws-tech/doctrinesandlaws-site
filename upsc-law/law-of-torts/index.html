Law of Torts: UPSC Law Optional notes and PYQs

All 13 syllabus units of Law of Torts, in syllabus order. Each unit page shows how to decode the question, frame the answer, which provisions and cases to cite, and every previous-year question from 2013 to 2026 (86 questions in all).

UPSC Law Optional desk · Paper II

Unit 1 · Asked 6 times · 2014–2025

Nature and definition of tort

Questions ask for the definition of tort, its distinction from crime and contract, and maxims.

Cases: Ashby v. White (1703), Gloucester Grammar School Case (1410), Mogul Steamship v. McGregor (1892)

Notes, answer frame and questions →Unit 2 · Asked 6 times · 2016–2024

Fault, strict and absolute liability

Questions compare strict and absolute liability. A table and the Oleum Gas rationale are essential.

Cases: Rylands v. Fletcher (1868), Read v. J. Lyons (1947), M.C. Mehta v. Union of India (1987)

Notes, answer frame and questions →Unit 3 · Asked 11 times · 2014–2026

Vicarious liability and State liability

Questions test vicarious liability's basis and State liability. Explain the policy rationale, then sovereign immunity.

Cases: Lloyd v. Grace, Smith & Co. (1912), Century Insurance v. Northern Ireland Road Transport Board (1942), Lister v. Hesley Hall (2001)

Notes, answer frame and questions →Unit 4 · Asked 5 times · 2014–2026

General defences in tort

Questions test volenti non fit injuria and its limits, and inevitable accident and act of God. Use the rescue and employment exceptions.

Cases: Smith v. Baker (1891), Hall v. Brooklands Auto-Racing Club (1933), Haynes v. Harwood (1935)

Notes, answer frame and questions →Unit 5 · Asked 3 times · 2015–2025

Joint tortfeasors

Questions ask who joint tortfeasors are and how liability is apportioned.

Cases: Brooke v. Bool (1928), The Koursk (1924), Merryweather v. Nixan (1799)

Notes, answer frame and questions →Unit 6 · Asked 5 times · 2018–2023

Remedies in tort

Questions test kinds of damages, remoteness and injunctions.

Cases: Rookes v. Barnard (1964), Re Polemis (1921), Overseas Tankship v. Morts Dock (Wagon Mound No. 1) (1961)

Notes, answer frame and questions →Unit 7 · Asked 11 times · 2013–2026

Negligence

Very frequent. Questions test res ipsa loquitur, medical negligence and contributory negligence, often as problems.

Cases: Donoghue v. Stevenson (1932), Caparo Industries v. Dickman (1990), Bolam v. Friern Hospital (1957)

Notes, answer frame and questions →Unit 8 · Asked 9 times · 2013–2025

Defamation (tort)

Questions test the elements and defences of defamation, and newer issues such as social media.

Cases: Hulton v. Jones (1910), Cassidy v. Daily Mirror (1929), Tolley v. J.S. Fry & Sons (1931)

Notes, answer frame and questions →Unit 9 · Asked 8 times · 2013–2026

Nuisance

Questions test public vs private nuisance, special damage and environmental links.

Cases: Attorney-General v. PYA Quarries (1957), Castle v. St. Augustine's Links (1922), Radhey Shyam v. Gur Prasad Sharma (1978)

Notes, answer frame and questions →Unit 10 · Not asked since 2013

Conspiracy (tort)

Rarely asked. Know the two forms and the predominant-purpose test.

Cases: Crofter Hand Woven Harris Tweed v. Veitch (1942), Quinn v. Leathem (1901)

Notes, answer frame and questions →Unit 11 · Asked 5 times · 2015–2024

False imprisonment

Questions test the elements of false imprisonment and public-law compensation.

Cases: Bird v. Jones (1845), Meering v. Grahame-White Aviation (1919), Herd v. Weardale Steel (1915)

Notes, answer frame and questions →Unit 12 · Asked 2 times · 2016–2023

Malicious prosecution

Know the five essentials and the line between malice and absence of probable cause.

Cases: Mohamed Amin v. Jogendra Kumar Bannerjee (1947, PC), West Bengal State Electricity Board v. Dilip Kumar Ray (2007), Willers v. Joyce (2016, UK SC)

Notes, answer frame and questions →Unit 13 · Asked 14 times · 2013–2026

Consumer Protection Act, 2019

Frequently asked. Questions test the definition of consumer, e-commerce, product liability and the CCPA. Contrast the 1986 and 2019 Acts.

Cases: Indian Medical Association v. V.P. Shantha (1995), Lucknow Development Authority v. M.K. Gupta (1994), Laxmi Engineering Works v. P.S.G. Industrial Institute (1995)
