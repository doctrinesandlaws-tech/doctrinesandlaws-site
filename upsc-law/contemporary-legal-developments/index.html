Contemporary Legal Developments: UPSC Law Optional notes and PYQs

All 8 syllabus units of Contemporary Legal Developments, in syllabus order. Each unit page shows how to decode the question, frame the answer, which provisions and cases to cite, and every previous-year question from 2013 to 2026 (80 questions in all).

UPSC Law Optional desk · Paper II

Unit 1 · Asked 9 times · 2013–2025

Public interest litigation

Questions test PIL's evolution, uses and misuse.

Cases: S.P. Gupta v. Union of India (1981), Bandhua Mukti Morcha v. Union of India (1984), People's Union for Democratic Rights v. Union of India (1982)

Notes, answer frame and questions →Unit 2 · Asked 18 times · 2013–2026

Intellectual property rights

Questions test IPR types and the balance between rights and public interest, especially copyright and patents.

Cases: R.G. Anand v. Delux Films (1978), Eastern Book Co. v. D.B. Modak (2008), University of Oxford v. Rameshwari Photocopy Services (Del. HC 2016)

Notes, answer frame and questions →Unit 3 · Asked 11 times · 2014–2026

IT Act and cyber law

Questions test the IT Act's framework, contraventions vs offences, intermediaries and data protection.

Cases: Shreya Singhal v. Union of India (2015), Avnish Bajaj v. State (NCT of Delhi) (2008), Anvar P.V. v. P.K. Basheer (2014)

Notes, answer frame and questions →Unit 4 · Asked 7 times · 2013–2026

Competition law

Questions test the shift from MRTP to the Competition Act, and digital markets.

Cases: CCI v. Steel Authority of India (2010), Excel Crop Care v. CCI (2017), CCI v. Bharti Airtel (2019)

Notes, answer frame and questions →Unit 5 · Asked 5 times · 2014–2026

Alternative dispute resolution

Questions test the types of ADR and their limits in public-law disputes.

Cases: Salem Advocate Bar Assn. v. Union of India (2005), Afcons Infrastructure v. Cherian Varkey Construction (2010), Patil Automation v. Rakheja Engineers (2022)

Notes, answer frame and questions →Unit 6 · Asked 14 times · 2013–2026

Environmental law statutes

Questions test statutes, principles (precautionary, polluter pays, public trust) and institutions such as the NGT.

Cases: Vellore Citizens' Welfare Forum v. Union of India (1996), A.P. Pollution Control Board v. M.V. Nayudu (1999), M.C. Mehta v. Kamal Nath (1997)

Notes, answer frame and questions →Unit 7 · Asked 10 times · 2013–2025

Right to Information Act, 2005

Questions test RTI's objectives, exemptions and recent amendments.

Cases: State of U.P. v. Raj Narain (1975), CBSE v. Aditya Bandopadhyay (2011), Girish Ramchandra Deshpande v. CIC (2012)

Notes, answer frame and questions →Unit 8 · Asked 6 times · 2014–2026

Trial by media

Questions test the conflict between fair trial and press freedom and how to balance it.

Cases: Sahara India Real Estate v. SEBI (2012), R.K. Anand v. Registrar, Delhi High Court (2009), Manu Sharma v. State (NCT of Delhi) (2010)
